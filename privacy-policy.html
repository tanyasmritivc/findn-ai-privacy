Privacy Policy for Findn AI

Last updated: January 26, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Findn AI collects only publicly visible information from LinkedIn and Instagram profiles that you choose to analyze. This includes:

  • Profile names and headlines
  • Job titles and company information
  • Public bio and description text
  • Publicly visible experience and skills

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected public profile information solely to:

  • Generate AI-powered networking insights and recommendations
  • Provide personalized conversation starters
  • Suggest professional growth opportunities
  • Improve the quality of our networking recommendations

3. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize your privacy and security:

  • No personal or private information is stored permanently
  • Profile data is processed temporarily for analysis only
  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS
  • We do not store login credentials or authentication information

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to third parties except:

  • OpenAI API for AI analysis (processed securely and not stored by OpenAI)
  • As required by law or to protect our rights

5. User Control

You have full control over your data:

  • You choose which profiles to analyze
  • You can uninstall the extension at any time
  • No data is collected without your explicit action

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal local storage only to:

  • Save your platform preferences (LinkedIn/Instagram toggles)
  • Remember your settings for better user experience

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
